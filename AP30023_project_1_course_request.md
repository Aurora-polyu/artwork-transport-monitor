# Project 1: Sotheby’s Fine art Auction

## Mission

Securing valuable fine art items for Sotheby’s auctions

## Context

There will be a Sotheby’s auction of fineart items in London and you are the Head of Logistics  
responsible for transporting art pieces in optimal condition from a designated origin to the  
destination, as well as avoiding any malicious activities during the journey. The art-pieces  
are each valued at 1B USD and attached with a sensor and 2D picture code.

## Basic Requirements

- Use appropriate sensors to show if the art-pieces are kept in a perfect environment in terms  
  of temperature and humidity. It is assumed that no intrusion ever happens if the art-pieces  
  are always kept in a dark environment.

- Use camera to recognize different 2D picture codes that show stories and origins of the fine  
  art items. Show at least 2 different art pieces.

- Use a webpage as a means of interaction to offer good user experience. The webpage includes  
  historical and real-time data to show that the sensors attached to the art piece are  
  functioning.
